# Wooden Accents Mod

**Vanilla-scale furniture and structural accents for every wood type.**

Wooden Accents is a building and decoration mod focused on adding the kinds of wooden blocks I kept wishing Minecraft had while building.

I like working with vanilla blocks, but sometimes a chair should just look like a chair, a support beam shouldn't require three layers of trapdoors, and having a few more shapes to work with can make a huge difference when you're trying to get a build to look right.

The goal isn't really to push one specific furniture style or completely change how Minecraft looks. Wooden Accents is meant to give you **more pieces to build with** while still fitting reasonably well alongside vanilla.

## What's Included?

Wooden Accents adds a mix of furniture, storage, and structural blocks, with variants for every vanilla wood type available in Minecraft 1.21.1.

### Furniture

* Chairs you can actually sit in
* Tables
* Coffee tables
* Desks
* Kitchen counters
* Kitchen cabinets
* Narrow bookshelves

Coffee tables can also be stacked into a taller version, so they can be used for more than just coffee tables. If you want to pick the tall version back up as-is, you can use Silk Touch.

### Storage

* Crates
* Desk drawers
* Kitchen cabinets
* Bracket shelves

**Crates** are basically my take on smaller wooden portable storage. They have nine inventory slots, keep their contents when broken, and can still stack as items when they're carrying things.

**Bracket shelves** can store and display up to three stacks of items directly on the shelf. Multiple shelves connect together visually, and powered shelves can swap their contents with parts of your hotbar. They also support comparator output and hopper interaction.

So yes, you can absolutely just use them to put some items on a shelf. I may have gotten slightly carried away after that, but I was inspired by Vanilla shelves.

### Building & Structural Blocks

* Stripped wood ladders
* Plank ladders
* Plank flooring
* Plank walls
* Picket fences
* Picket fence gates
* Support beams
* Thin pillars
* Thick pillars

The **stripped wood ladders** are 3D ladders that connect vertically, while **plank ladders** are a simpler design that basically looks like somebody nailed a few planks to the wall.

**Support beams** can connect in every direction and are useful for framing builds or adding exposed structural details. Visually, they're kind of like the wooden pipes you see in some mods, except they're just featureless structural pieces rather than actual pipes.

The thin and thick pillars are basically another option for vertical supports, and they automatically connect with matching pillars above and below.

## Compatibility

Wooden Accents currently supports:

* **Minecraft 1.21.1**
* **Fabric or NeoForge**
* **Java 21+**
* Client and dedicated servers

### Required Dependencies

* [VoxLib 1.8.0+1.21.1](https://modrinth.com/mod/voxlib) for your loader
* Fabric: [Fabric API 0.116.17+1.21.1](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and [Fabric Language Kotlin 1.14.1+kotlin.2.4.20](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-language-kotlin)
* NeoForge: [NeoForge 21.1.251](https://neoforged.net/) and [Kotlin for Forge 5.12.0](https://modrinth.com/mod/kotlin-for-forge)

If you're installing through the Modrinth App, it should handle these dependencies for you anyway.

### Recommended Mods

Wooden Accents doesn't require these, but they make a few parts of the mod nicer to use:

* [Shulker Box Tooltip](https://modrinth.com/mod/shulkerboxtooltip) or [Easy Shulker Boxes](https://modrinth.com/mod/easy-shulker-boxes) for previewing crate inventories
* [Just Enough Items](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) or [Roughly Enough Items](https://modrinth.com/mod/rei) for looking up recipes

## Version Support & What's Next

**Minecraft 1.21.1 is the current supported version of Wooden Accents.** It has Fabric and NeoForge builds. Older 1.20.1 and 1.20.6 builds remain available for older worlds.

`1.21.1-1.2.0.0` is the current stable release on this line. The next focus is new content such as modular bridges and benches, plus keeping older Minecraft lines on maintenance branches.

I don't plan to maintain many active Minecraft versions at once. Once development moves forward, older versions will stay available and receive only important fixes.

After the port, I do have ideas for more stuff, including things like **modular bridges and benches**, but the roadmap is intentionally more of a direction than a promise. Plans tend to change once I actually start implementing them.

I'd rather have one current version that feels finished than several half-maintained versions.

## AI Usage

Wooden Accents was originally created and developed by me without generative AI. More recent development has included AI-assisted tools for parts of the code, testing, debugging, refactoring, documentation, and other development work.

AI is still just a development tool for me. I decide what gets added or changed, direct the implementation, review and test the results, and redo or reject things when they don't turn out how I want.

The majority of this codebase is still code written by hand by me.
